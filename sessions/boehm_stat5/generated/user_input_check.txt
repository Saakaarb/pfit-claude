## Warnings

1. Very wide search ranges. All six trainable rate constants are searched over
   [1e-5, 1e5] (ten orders of magnitude) on a log scale. This is the range
   specified by the PEtab benchmark and is correctly log-scaled, but it is a
   large space and the published optimum sits near the box edges (k_imp_homo and
   k_phos are O(1e5), near the upper bound; k_exp_hetero is O(1e-5), near the
   lower bound). The global search must therefore reach the corners of the box;
   the DE budget (300 x 30 = 9000 evaluations) is adequate, but a poor fit would
   most likely mean a parameter pinned at a bound and worth re-examining.

2. specC17 is unpacked inside user_defined_system but only used in the
   observables (loss / writeout), not in the ODE right-hand side. This is
   harmless and needs no change.
