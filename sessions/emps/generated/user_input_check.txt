Warnings
--------
1. `np.sign` is not in lib/LLM/api/jax.md, though `jnp.sign` does exist in the
   pinned jax 0.6.2 (verified by introspection). The digest lists 24 jnp names
   and sign is not among them; neither is `maximum`, which sessions/cascaded_
   tanks needs for its clamped square root. The digest declares itself the only
   authority - "anything not in them does not exist in the pinned versions" -
   so as written it cannot answer the question this model asks. Nothing is
   broken and the translation will work, but the digest's curated function list
   is incomplete rather than authoritative. Fix by adding sign/maximum/minimum
   to the include list in tools/gen_api_context.yaml and regenerating; this is a
   tooling change, outside this session.

2. Runtime. The record is 24841 rows, so every candidate solve carries 24841
   save points, and the population stage runs population_size x num_iters =
   100 x 30 = 3000 of them, each collapsing its step at 7 velocity reversals.
   If that proves too slow, decimating the CSV to 5 ms still resolves the
   physical time constant M/Fv = 0.467 s by a factor of 93, and 4969 rows is
   ample for 4 parameters. Decimation discards real measurements, so it is a
   deliberate trade rather than a free win.

3. v(0) = 0 is an assumption - the velocity is never measured. It is well
   justified here (the record begins with the axis at its origin at rest, and
   q(0) matches data row 0 exactly per D10), but this is regime 1, so an error
   in it would be an irreducible loss floor rather than something the
   parameters could absorb.

RESOLUTION APPLIED (second pass)
--------------------------------
R1's conflict was resolved by changing the MODEL, not the box: sign(v) is now
tanh(v/v_eps) with v_eps = 1.0e-04 m/s fixed, the measurement's own velocity
resolution. R3 was NOT applied - M and Fv keep their four-decade boxes.

Consequences, re-derived:
- the RHS is now smooth everywhere, so the chattering axis of R1 falls away;
- the friction term contributes a new timescale M*v_eps/Fc: 4.7e-04 s at the
  reference values, 4.7e-08 s at the corner (M small, Fc large), 4.7 s at the
  opposite corner. That is on top of the slaved v mode at Fv/M up to
  2.1e+04 /s;
- both new modes are smooth and slaved, so `integrator` moved Dopri5 ->
  Kvaerno5. An explicit method would now be stability-limited at ~5e+08 steps
  at the stiff corner;
- stepsize_atol on v is 1.0e-07 (population) and 1.0e-09 (gradient), i.e. 1e-03
  and 1e-05 of the smoothing width, so the transition layer is resolved;
- parameters recovered here are only approximately comparable to the published
  reference values, since the model is no longer the published one.

R3 below is therefore superseded and is retained only as the record of the
alternative that was not taken. R1 stands as the analysis that forced the
choice; its "keep Dopri5" verdict was conditional on R3 and no longer applies.

Recommendations
---------------
[R3] Narrow the M and Fv boxes to [v/10, v*10]
     Evidence: only the RATIO Fv/M enters the Jacobian, whose eigenvalues are
          exactly 0 and -Fv/M (J = [[0, 1], [0, -Fv/M]], since sign(v) has zero
          derivative away from v = 0). Both parameters currently carry the
          [v/100, v*100] box, so their ratio spans EIGHT decades:
          Fv/M in [2.1e-04, 2.1e+04] /s, against a physical reference value of
          203.5/95.1 = 2.14 /s. At the top corner an explicit method needs
          24.84 * 2.15e+04 = 5.3e+05 steps for stability; at the reference value
          it needs 53. Narrowing both to one decade caps the ratio at 214 /s and
          the step count at 5.3e+03.
     Why: this is the whole reason the solver choice is contested (see R1). The
          eight-decade ratio box is an artifact of applying the four-decade rule
          independently to two parameters that only ever appear as a quotient -
          it is not a statement about the hardware. Narrowing it removes the
          stiff corner, leaves the problem purely non-smooth, and makes the
          explicit solver unambiguously correct.
     Edit: model.trainable_parameters, M entry  -> min_val: 9.51e+00, max_val: 9.51e+02
           model.trainable_parameters, Fv entry -> min_val: 2.04e+01, max_val: 2.04e+03

[R1] integrator: Dopri5 -> keep Dopri5, but only if R3 is applied
     Evidence: two axes, and as the box stands they conflict.
          Non-smoothness: sign(v) is a CHATTERING switch, not a one-way one -
          the velocity reverses 7 times in this record, and each reversal
          re-crosses it. An implicit inner solve cannot converge across a
          discontinuity for ANY max_steps, so raising it is not a remedy.
          Stiffness: the v mode IS slaved - f_v = (F - Fv*v)/M tends to 0 as the
          velocity reaches quasi-equilibrium F/Fv while df_v/dv = -Fv/M stays
          finite - and over the current box that mode reaches 2.1e+04 /s.
          Stiff AND chattering is the one case R1 says neither family handles,
          and it instructs saying so plainly rather than picking a lesser evil.
          The conflict is a property of the BOX, not of the machine: at the
          reference values the mode is 2.14 /s, entirely benign.
     Why: with R3 applied there is no fast slaved mode left (worst case 214 /s,
          5.3e+03 explicit steps against max_steps = 100000), so the stiffness
          axis falls away and the remaining non-smoothness points squarely at an
          explicit method - the branch of R1 no other session in this repo
          reaches. Without R3 the honest answer is that neither Dopri5 nor
          Kvaerno5 is right and the model would have to change instead:
          replacing sign(v) with tanh(v / v_eps) over a physically meaningful
          velocity width would restore the implicit solver the stiff corner
          demands, at the cost of no longer fitting the published reference
          model. R3 is the cheaper resolution and preserves the comparison.
     Edit: none, conditional on R3 being applied.
